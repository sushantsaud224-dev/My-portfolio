About me

Learning by designing, building, and experimenting.

My work is about turning curiosity into creation.

I bring tools, ideas, and practical STEAM activities into learning spaces so students can make and understand real things.

I’m Shekhar Ram Saud, a STEAM educator and maker based in Godawari-02, Attariya, Kailali. Since 2080, I have worked with more than 30 schools across Sudurpashchim, helping students explore ideas through practical projects and real components.

I’m the CEO of Godawari Aawiskar Kendra and proprietor of 3D and EV Center, Attariya. My work includes CAD design, electronics, machine operation, making device components, and programming machines and real-world devices.

I enjoy supporting students as they move from an idea to a design, from a design to a prototype, and from a prototype to something that works.
